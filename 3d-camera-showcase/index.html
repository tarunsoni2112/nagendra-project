Canon AT-1

Legendary 35mm SLR Camera

Experience the classic Canon AT-1, a manual focus 35mm single-lens reflex (SLR) camera introduced in 1977. Known for its reliability, precision, and timeless design.

Key Features

Discover what makes the Canon AT-1 a photographer's favorite

Manual Control

Full manual exposure control gives photographers complete creative freedom.

Match Needle Metering

Center-weighted TTL metering system with match-needle display in viewfinder.

Interchangeable Lenses

Compatible with the entire range of Canon FD mount lenses.

Mechanical Shutter

Reliable mechanical shutter with speeds from 1/1000 to 1 second plus Bulb.
